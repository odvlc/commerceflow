# Synthetic demonstration return policy

## Return window

You may request a return for unused items in their original packaging within 30 calendar days of delivery.
This fictional policy is for the CommerceFlow demo. The application does not process returns.

## Damaged items

For a damaged item, contact the demonstration support team with the order identifier. Eligibility requires review.

## Refunds

Do not promise a refund or initiate a payment action. Payments in CommerceFlow are simulated.
